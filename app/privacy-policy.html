Nukkad — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 03, 2025
Nukkad is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Nukkad mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide directly

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Delivery address

b) Automatically collected information

  • Device and app usage data
  • Store interactions and browsing patterns

We collect this information to improve your shopping experience and ensure accurate service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data may be used for:

  • Processing orders
  • Communicating with you
  • Providing delivery updates
  • Improving app performance & personalization
  • Fraud prevention & security
  • Customer support & issue resolution
  • Sending notifications, promotions, or updates
We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Sharing Information With Third Parties

We may share limited data with:

  • Local stores to fulfill your orders

All third parties are required to protect your data responsibly.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

5. Data Retention

We retain user data as long as necessary for:

  • Order history
  • Support needs
  • Legal or compliance requirements

You can request data deletion at any time.

6. Account Deletion

Upon requesting account deletion:

  • All personal data, order history, and stored information will be erased
  • We will contact you to confirm the deletion request
  • After final confirmation, the process is irreversible

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update the Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted in-app. Continued use indicates acceptance of these updates.
